# Millie (The Overwhelmed Beginner)

**The Wound:** Imposter syndrome. She feels the weight of her family's farming legacy but lacks practical experience.
**The Arc:** Moving from relying on textbooks to trusting her instincts and the land.

## Dialogue Flow

```mermaid
graph TD
    Phase1[Phase 1: Panic] --> Phase2[Phase 2: Relief]

    Q1>"""Oh! Please don't step there! I think I planted... carrots? Or maybe radishes. The book said they need loamy soil, but I don't know what loam looks like! It just looks like dirt! Everything is just dirt and I'm ruining it!"""]
    Phase1 -.- Q1

    Q2>"""Look! They sprouted! I mean, they're a little crooked, and something took a bite out of that cabbage... but it grew! I actually did it. Do you think Clementine could use these for the bakery? They're not perfect, but they're ours."""]
    Phase2 -.- Q2

    Q3>"""I threw the books out. Well, I put them in the shed. Turns out, if you just pay attention, the soil tells you what it needs. Old Ben even asked me for advice on his pasture grass today. Can you believe that?"""]
    Phase3 -.- Q3
```
